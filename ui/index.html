No proposal leaves unchecked

Obstat writes research proposals and holds each one until a separate Critic has checked every claim against the person it names.

The committed run wrote 3 proposals and turned 135 challenges away, in 23 model calls.

Nihil obstat: the censor’s line that let a book go to press. Obstat prints it only after every claim checks out.

First, a writer writes.

From the researcher’s profile, as the profile states it. Writer 2 worked from R0139, whose profile lists SYN-PUB-01827.

Then the gate holds it.

Only that writer’s loop waits. Writers 1 and 3 kept working through the hold.

Next, the Critic checks.

Ten checks, run against the records themselves. The paper’s author entry carries R0539, not R0139.

Last, it passes or returns.

A failure goes back with its reasons. In round 2 Writer 2 cited R0539, and pair-2 was written.

Ten checks on every claim

  • Fails when a cited ID is not in the dataset, or when the challenge belongs to another company.

  • Fails when the record’s author, inventor or participant entries do not carry the named researcher. In the run: SYN-PUB-01827’s author entries carry R0539, not R0139.

  • Fails when a claim names a second record of one person. In the run: SYN-PUB-01827 is attributed to R0539. R0139 and R0539 look like one person (reversed name order, same faculty). Cite R0539 or state both IDs.

  • Fails when the evidence belongs to a different person with the same name. In the dataset: SYN-PUB-09418 belongs to R0881 Nina Mamužić (Faculty of Agriculture), not R0128 (Faculty of Technology).

  • Fails without a quote from the researcher’s own open_to or notes, or when they left, or are emeritus and the role is not consulting. In the run: Quote the words in R0906’s open_to or notes that support this role.

  • Fails when commitments the gate has already passed exceed what the researcher’s note allows.

  • Fails when the Industry Analyst turned the challenge away as not research, or as having no fitting researcher.

  • Fails when a required section is missing, a marker points at no claim, or a claim is a pasted paragraph.

  • Fails on worth language about people: best researcher, top-ranked, scores next to names.

  • Fails when a small model, given only the cited records, finds they do not support the sentence.
