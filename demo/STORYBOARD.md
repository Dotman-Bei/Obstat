# Obstat demo film: storyboard

**Length** 2:09 · **Picture** 1920×1080, 30 fps · **Voice** Gemini TTS, Charon · **Score** original, Lyria RealTime · **Footage** real recordings of the product (local build of obstat.vercel.app), nothing mocked.

## Techniques taken from the reference

| Reference technique | How this film uses it |
|---|---|
| Problem first, product second; a 2 s brand card as the hook | 3.8 s mark animation, then the real data problem before Obstat is named |
| Camera moves land on the noun being spoken (~400 ms eases, 2–4 s holds) | Every zoom is keyed to a voiceover word: "two IDs", "Writer 2 is held", "claim four", "round two" |
| Three zoom levels: full, ~1.3×, ~1.8× | 1.0× to orient, 1.25–1.55× for a panel, 1.75–2.2× for the one line that matters |
| Clean cursor with eased paths and a click ring | The real mouse path, recorded and redrawn as a crisp arrow with a soft teal ring on click; it hides itself when idle |
| Speed ramps instead of jump cuts | The 75 s replay plays at 5×, 1.6× around the hold, then 12×; idle waits are compressed, the moments that matter play at 1× |
| Sentence captions at the bottom | One sentence at a time, small, on a white pill |
| Calm, measured narration with pauses; ~-7 dB music ducking | Charon at a conversational pace; the score dips under every line and rises between |
| Restrained UI sound | Four sounds in total: a trackpad click, a wooden tock, one low thunk for the hold, a soft air swish at the two act changes |
| End card: logo, line, URL | Lockup, "No proposal leaves unchecked.", obstat.vercel.app, credit line |

Not taken: glitch and whip transitions, glow, a dark neon palette, a roadmap teaser. The film stays in the product's own stone, ink and teal, and only shows features that carry the story.

## Mix

Voice at full level, loudness-matched per line. Score at -9 dB, dipping to about -18 dB under every line with 0.4 s ramps. Clicks at -10 dB, the other effects between -6 and -15 dB. Final pass normalised to -14 LUFS for web playback.
