# Mitigation of Rapid Membrane Fouling in Dairy Wastewater Treatment Systems

**Pair** pair-3 · **Researcher** Ivona Bogdanović (R0269), Faculty of Technology · **Company** ReVita Water (C088) · **Challenge** C088-CH2 · **Format** Joint project

## Problem

ReVita Water services two dairy plants near Temerin and Bečej where membrane bioreactor membranes foul faster than design assumptions, requiring full cleaning-in-place every 5 to 7 days instead of every 12 to 14 days [2]. Each full CIP consumes roughly 200 litres of citric acid and caustic solution and takes the system offline for 4 to 6 hours, resulting in significant chemical costs and lost treatment capacity over a year [2]. Seasonal patterns show fouling is worst in summer, while high fats, proteins, and sugars form a persistent gel layer [2].

## Research questions

1. What are the precise chemical and structural compositions of the foulant deposit layers accumulating on membranes at the dairy sites during summer and winter operations?
2. How do temperature variations and dairy wastewater constituents influence gel layer formation and transmembrane pressure spikes in membrane bioreactor systems?
3. Which adjustments to pre-treatment, aeration strategies, or cleaning protocols can reliably extend membrane cleaning intervals to at least twelve days?
